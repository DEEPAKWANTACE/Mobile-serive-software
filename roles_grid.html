Front-desk customer touchpoint. Registers walk-in job sheets, takes advance, makes approval calls, sends WhatsApp alerts, collects balances, and hands over delivered devices.

Branch inventory custodian. Fulfills engineer part requests, marks stock out, logs part inward receipts, adjustments, and branch-to-branch inventory transfers.

ACCOUNTSUser: accounts

Financial audit and cash counter verification. Reconciles Cash, UPI, and Card transactions in the Day Book, tracks office expenses, and verifies revenue balances.
